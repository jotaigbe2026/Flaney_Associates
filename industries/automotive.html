Automotive & Transportation

Materials substitution and lightweighting decisions with warranty exposure attached, plus the aerospace-grade requirements that increasingly follow them.

Discuss Your Challenge Get the Checklist

Where the practice works in this sector

  • Metal-to-polymer and metal-to-composite substitution and its failure modes.
  • Under-bonnet and battery-system polymers — thermal, chemical and electrical service conditions.
  • Aircraft and aerospace structures and components.
  • Durability, creep and fatigue over a design life measured in years, not test cycles.

Questions that typically call for an outside view

  • What failure modes does replacing a metal part with a polymer or composite introduce?
  • Will this polymer survive under-bonnet or battery-system temperatures, fluids and electrical demands?
  • How will the part creep, fatigue and age over a design life measured in years?
  • Why is a component failing in the field when it passed validation testing?
  • Is a supplier’s material change behind a new pattern of warranty claims?

How the practice is engaged

  • Materials Selection & Qualification — choosing and validating a material.
  • Failure & Root-Cause Analysis — why it failed, and what is defensible.
  • Product Development & Materials Innovation — concept through production.

Every engagement starts with a short conversation with the principal and a written scope before any work begins. If the problem belongs with your own team or your supplier’s technical service group, you will be told so.
