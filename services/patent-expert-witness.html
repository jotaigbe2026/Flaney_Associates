Materials-Science Expert for Patent Matters

Technical analysis for infringement, invalidity and prior-art questions in polymers, composites and related materials.

Request a Conflict Check Expert Witness Overview

Matters this covers

  • Infringement — whether an accused material, formulation or process falls within the claims, including testing where the claim turns on a measured property.
  • Invalidity — whether the claimed invention was anticipated or would have been obvious to a person of ordinary skill in the art.
  • Prior art — searching and reading the scientific, patent and trade literature in the relevant materials area.
  • Patentability and prosecution support — patentability research, and technical support on initial filings, valuations and prosecution or defence before the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office.

The technical questions that usually decide the matter

  • What would a person of ordinary skill in this field have understood a claim term to mean at the priority date?
  • Does the accused product actually have the claimed composition, structure or property, and by which test method?
  • Does a prior publication disclose every element of the claim, or only something close to it?
  • Does the specification teach enough to make and use the claimed material without undue experimentation?

Where the product under evaluation cannot be assessed with standard equipment, custom test equipment can be acquired or built to suit it. That is often what produces the measurement an infringement question depends on.

Why this sits inside the principal’s field

The principal is a named inventor on seven patents and the author of more than 150 refereed publications, so he reads claims and prior art from both sides: as someone who has written them and as someone whose own work has been examined against them. Four decades of teaching also bear directly on explaining polymer chemistry to a court without distorting it.
